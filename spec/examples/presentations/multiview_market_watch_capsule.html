Critical Minerals Watch Capsule

A compact market-watch fixture for testing whether investment-style highlights work as reader, mobile feed, slides, and reel surfaces.

Watch thesis

The artifact tracks early-stage critical minerals themes where land position, permitting path, infrastructure, and technical evidence matter more than promotional claims.

Signals to monitor

Look for drill-ready targets, coherent geophysical signatures, credible sampling, nearby infrastructure, aligned management incentives, and repeatable disclosure quality.

Risk frame

The main risks are dilution, weak field execution, unclear metallurgy, inaccessible terrain, over-promoted analogies, and limited capital-market attention.

Vault workflow

Each new company capsule should preserve the original source bytes, extract plain text, attach tags, and link siblings by project, commodity, region, and source registry.

Decision use

Use the capsule as a watchlist memory object, not as a buy signal. It should make follow-up questions clearer and keep provenance visible.
