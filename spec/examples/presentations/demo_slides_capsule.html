One Capsule, Declared Slides

This demo keeps the primary meaning in the reader layer and also declares a paged desktop slide presentation at #capsule-slides. The slide deck is not inferred from layout; it is advertised by the manifest.

Set up the idea

Capsules can declare a slide presentation without making slides the only readable content.

Keep a slide shape

The slide surface uses a stable 16:9 frame and paged navigation semantics.

Preserve meaning

The reader layer keeps the primary meaning available without JavaScript.

Let hosts choose

Vault or another host can offer this view because it is declared in the manifest.
