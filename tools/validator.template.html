Verifies the five required blocks, the manifest, the absence of external references, and integrity.content_hash — recomputed with the canonicalization recipe that the capsule's declared spec_version selects (recipe v1 for 0.1–0.3, RFC 8785 for 0.4).

This is a focused integrity checker, not the full reference validator: compiler/validate.py runs the complete conformance suite including capability truthfulness, presentations, and progressive enhancement.
